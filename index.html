Abstract

Temporally consistent depth estimation is necessary for precise omnidirectional 3D reconstruction. Existing omnidirectional stereo matching methods achieve high per-frame accuracy, but their frame-wise processing limits temporal consistency. We propose TC-Omni, the first OSM framework that predicts temporally consistent omnidirectional depth from sequential inputs.

TC-Omni enriches spatial and temporal representations through multi-view context enhancement (MVCE), temporal context enhancement (TCEM), and temporal correlation learning (TCLM). A confidence-based temporal gradient matching loss further improves consistency by emphasizing reliable regions and suppressing ambiguous temporal supervision. Experiments on synthetic and real-world sequences demonstrate state-of-the-art depth accuracy and temporal consistency.

Why Temporal Consistency?

Applying sequential inputs to existing frame-based OSM models does not guarantee stable geometry. TC-Omni explicitly models temporal relationships and reduces both depth error and frame-to-frame inconsistency.

Method

Sequential features from four fisheye cameras are projected into the equirectangular domain through spherical sweeping. MVCE integrates complementary views, while TCEM and TCLM enhance temporal context and correlation features. A 3D ConvGRU iteratively estimates the final omnidirectional depth sequence.

Spatio-Temporal Representation Enhancement

MVCE alleviates viewpoint ambiguity using all camera views. TCEM injects temporal information into the context branch, and TCLM learns temporally coherent correlations before recurrent depth refinement.

OmniScape Dataset

We collected OmniScape, a real-world sequential omnidirectional stereo dataset, using our mobile data acquisition platform equipped with four wide-FOV fisheye cameras and a 32-channel LiDAR sensor. OmniScape provides synchronized multi-view fisheye sequences and LiDAR depth measurements for evaluating depth accuracy and temporal consistency under practical sensor noise and challenging outdoor conditions.

Qualitative Results

On Sunny sequences, TC-Omni preserves thin structures and nearby object boundaries across consecutive frames more reliably than frame-wise and sequential baselines.

Real-World Results

On our collected OmniScape dataset, TC-Omni maintains stable object boundaries despite real-world sensor noise, demonstrating robust temporal modeling beyond synthetic data.
